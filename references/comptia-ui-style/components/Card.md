# Card

White content card that lifts off the page with `shadow-card`; the main building block of every section. Structure: optional Eyebrow, `h3` title, one short paragraph, one button. Use `pw-card`; use `pw-card-link` when the whole card is clickable. To group cards, put them on a `pw-panel` (grey `surface-muted`). Do not add borders or colored edges. You provide the content.
